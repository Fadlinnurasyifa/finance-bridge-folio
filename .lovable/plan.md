# Premium Accounting & Finance Portfolio

## Goal
Create a responsive, single-page portfolio that immediately positions Fadlin Nur Asyifa as an Accounting & Finance professional with a credible technology advantage.

## What I’ll build
- A refined corporate visual system using off-white, deep navy, charcoal, muted blue, strong typography, restrained borders, and generous whitespace.
- Sticky navigation with smooth scrolling across Home, About, Experience, Projects, Skills, Certifications, Education, and Contact.
- A high-impact opening section with professional positioning, opportunity badge, and experience/project/CV actions.
- Finance-first About section with a clear Finance versus Technology capability comparison.
- Professional experience timeline, quantified impact indicators, and prior finance administration experience.
- Expandable project case-study cards for AR aging, bank reconciliation, marketplace settlement, journals, and a realistic demonstration finance dashboard.
- Categorized skills, certifications, current learning, education equation, career direction, achievements, and contact sections.
- Subtle entrance and interaction animations with reduced-motion support.
- Search and social metadata using the supplied title and description.

## Content assumptions
- Use the supplied copy and facts without inventing employers, credentials, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Label all dashboard numbers as sample or demonstration data.
- Since no email, LinkedIn URL, or CV file was provided, contact and download controls will be clearly prepared but non-deceptive until real details are supplied.

## Technical details
- Build on the existing TanStack Start route at `/`.
- Define semantic design tokens and reusable visual utilities in the global Tailwind v4 stylesheet.
- Use accessible native disclosure patterns for expandable projects and semantic sections/headings.
- Verify desktop and mobile rendering, interactions, page metadata, and preview health.
